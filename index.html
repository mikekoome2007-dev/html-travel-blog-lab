Adventure in Fire Island

My trip to Fire island National park was an incredible escape into nature.The island offers beautiful ocean beaches, historical landmarks like the fire island lighthouse and maritime sunken forest.My best part was watching the evening sunset over the dunes and watching the harbour seals and dolphins at the afternoon.
